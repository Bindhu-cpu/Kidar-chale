TRAVEL ITINERARY AND PLANNING GUIDE

GENERAL ITINERARY STRUCTURE

A good travel itinerary should organize the trip into:
Morning
Afternoon
Evening

The itinerary should consider:
- Destination
- Trip duration
- Traveller preferences
- Budget
- Travel time between places
- Opening and closing timings
- Distance between attractions
- Meal breaks
- Rest time
- Weather and season

DAY 1 PLANNING

The first day should generally contain nearby attractions and easy activities because travellers may need time to check in and settle down.

Suggested structure:
Morning: Travel to destination or hotel check-in.
Afternoon: Visit a nearby attraction.
Evening: Explore a local market, beach, viewpoint or food area.

GOA ITINERARY GUIDE

For a 3-day Goa trip:

Day 1:
Morning: Reach Goa and check in to accommodation.
Afternoon: Visit Calangute Beach and Baga Beach.
Evening: Enjoy sunset at Baga or nearby beach and explore local food options.

Day 2:
Morning: Visit Fort Aguada.
Afternoon: Explore Anjuna and Vagator.
Evening: Enjoy sunset and explore nearby cafes or nightlife areas.

Day 3:
Morning: Visit Basilica of Bom Jesus and explore Panjim.
Afternoon: Explore local markets and try Goan food.
Evening: Relax at a beach and prepare for departure.

For a 4-day Goa trip:
Day 4 can include additional beaches, water activities, shopping or relaxation depending on traveller preferences.

ITINERARY OPTIMIZATION RULES

Attractions that are close to each other should preferably be grouped together on the same day.

Long-distance attractions should not be unnecessarily combined with multiple distant attractions on the same day.

Morning activities should preferably include sightseeing or outdoor activities.

Afternoon activities can include museums, shopping, restaurants or indoor attractions.

Evening activities can include beaches, viewpoints, markets, cafes, nightlife or relaxing activities.

The itinerary should leave enough time for meals and rest.

The itinerary should avoid excessive travel between attractions.

Budget-friendly itineraries should prioritize free or low-cost attractions.

Adventure-focused itineraries should allocate more time for activities.

Relaxation-focused itineraries should contain fewer attractions and more free time.

Family itineraries should prioritize comfortable travel, safe activities and family-friendly attractions.

Group itineraries should consider shared transportation and activities suitable for multiple people.

WEATHER AND SEASON

Travel plans should consider the best season for the destination.

Outdoor activities may need alternative plans during heavy rain or unsuitable weather.

Snow destinations may have seasonal accessibility restrictions.

Travel timings and attraction availability can change, so the final itinerary should verify current information when real-time data is available.

TRAVEL TIME RULE

The travel time between two attractions should be considered before adding both attractions to the same itinerary.

Nearby attractions can be combined efficiently.

If two locations are far apart, they should preferably be placed on different parts of the itinerary.

FINAL ITINERARY FORMAT

A final travel plan should contain:

Destination
Trip duration
Estimated budget
Best time to visit
Places to visit
Day 1 plan
Day 2 plan
Day 3 plan
Additional days if required
Transportation suggestions
Food suggestions
Nearby places
Travel tips
Budget breakdown

The final itinerary should be personalized according to the user's budget, interests, duration, number of travellers and travel style.
