DESTINATION KNOWLEDGE BASE

GOA
Goa is a popular beach destination in India.
Best time to visit Goa is from October to March.
Popular places include Baga Beach, Calangute Beach, Anjuna Beach, Vagator Beach, Fort Aguada, Panjim and Basilica of Bom Jesus.
Activities include beach visits, water sports, sunset views, nightlife, sightseeing and local food.
Baga and Calangute are suitable for beaches and water activities.
Anjuna and Vagator are known for scenic beaches and sunset experiences.
Fort Aguada is a historical attraction.
Panjim is suitable for exploring Portuguese-influenced architecture and local markets.
Basilica of Bom Jesus is a famous historical and religious attraction.
Goa is suitable for beach, nightlife, food, relaxation and adventure trips.

MANALI
Manali is a mountain destination in Himachal Pradesh.
Best time to visit Manali depends on the activity. March to June is suitable for pleasant weather and outdoor activities. December to February is suitable for snow experiences.
Popular places include Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, Hadimba Temple, Mall Road, Old Manali and Vashisht.
Activities include snow activities, sightseeing, trekking, mountain views, shopping and cafe visits.
Solang Valley is suitable for adventure activities and mountain scenery.
Hadimba Temple is a historical and cultural attraction.
Mall Road is suitable for shopping and local food.
Old Manali is popular for cafes, nature and relaxed experiences.
Manali is suitable for mountains, nature, adventure, relaxation and snow experiences.

OOTY
Ooty is a hill station in Tamil Nadu.
Best time to visit Ooty is generally from October to June.
Popular places include Ooty Lake, Botanical Garden, Doddabetta Peak, Rose Garden and Tea Gardens.
Activities include boating, sightseeing, nature walks, photography and exploring tea plantations.
Ooty Lake is suitable for boating and relaxing.
Doddabetta Peak provides mountain and valley views.
The Botanical Garden is suitable for nature lovers.
Tea gardens are suitable for scenic views and photography.
Ooty is suitable for nature, mountains, relaxation and family trips.

JAIPUR
Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and is known for forts, palaces and historical architecture.
Best time to visit Jaipur is generally from October to March.
Popular places include Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and Jal Mahal.
Activities include sightseeing, photography, shopping, cultural experiences and local food.
Amber Fort is suitable for history and architecture.
Hawa Mahal is a famous historical landmark.
City Palace provides cultural and architectural experiences.
Jantar Mantar is a historical astronomical observatory.
Jaipur is suitable for history, culture, food, photography and shopping.

KERALA
Kerala is known for beaches, backwaters, hills, wildlife and nature.
Popular destinations include Munnar, Alleppey, Kochi, Varkala and Thekkady.
Best time to visit depends on the destination, but October to March is generally comfortable for many tourist activities.
Munnar is suitable for mountains, tea gardens and nature.
Alleppey is famous for backwaters and houseboat experiences.
Kochi is suitable for history, culture and food.
Varkala is suitable for beaches and relaxation.
Thekkady is suitable for wildlife and nature experiences.
Kerala is suitable for nature, beaches, relaxation, food, culture and adventure.

HYDERABAD
Hyderabad is a major city in Telangana known for history, food and modern attractions.
Popular places include Charminar, Golconda Fort, Hussain Sagar Lake, Salar Jung Museum and Necklace Road.
Activities include sightseeing, historical exploration, shopping and food experiences.
Charminar is a famous historical landmark.
Golconda Fort is suitable for history and architecture.
Salar Jung Museum is suitable for art and history.
Hyderabad is especially suitable for history, food, culture and short city trips.
