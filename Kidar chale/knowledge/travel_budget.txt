TRAVEL AND BUDGET KNOWLEDGE BASE

GENERAL TRAVEL COST CATEGORIES

A travel budget can be divided into:
1. Transportation
2. Accommodation
3. Food
4. Local transportation
5. Activities and entry fees
6. Shopping and miscellaneous expenses

The total trip cost depends on destination, number of travellers, trip duration, travel season and travel style.

BUDGET TRAVEL STYLE
Budget travel focuses on affordable hotels, hostels, public transportation, local food and low-cost activities.
It is suitable for students and travellers who want to reduce expenses.

BALANCED TRAVEL STYLE
Balanced travel combines comfortable accommodation, affordable restaurants, convenient transportation and selected paid activities.

COMFORTABLE TRAVEL STYLE
Comfortable travel includes better hotels, private or convenient transportation, restaurants and multiple paid activities.

LUXURY TRAVEL STYLE
Luxury travel may include premium hotels, private transportation, premium restaurants, guided experiences and higher-cost activities.

GOA BUDGET INFORMATION

Goa can be planned for different budgets depending on accommodation and transportation choices.

Budget accommodation may include hostels and affordable hotels.
Mid-range accommodation may include comfortable hotels and resorts.
Luxury accommodation may include premium resorts and hotels.

Food costs depend on whether travellers choose local restaurants, cafes or premium restaurants.

Local transportation options include buses, rental scooters, taxis and app-based cab services where available.

Common activities include beaches, forts, water sports, sightseeing, nightlife and local food experiences.

Typical cost categories for Goa:
Accommodation: approximately ₹800–₹6000+ per person per night depending on accommodation type.
Food: approximately ₹300–₹1500+ per person per day depending on food choices.
Local transportation: approximately ₹200–₹1000+ per person per day depending on transport.
Activities: approximately ₹200–₹2000+ depending on activity.

BUDGET CALCULATION

Estimated total trip cost can be calculated using:

Total Cost =
Transportation
+ Accommodation
+ Food
+ Local Transportation
+ Activities
+ Miscellaneous Expenses

The estimated cost should increase when:
- Number of travellers increases
- Trip duration increases
- Premium accommodation is selected
- Private transportation is selected
- More paid activities are included

The estimated cost can decrease when:
- Budget accommodation is selected
- Public transportation is used
- Local food is selected
- Free attractions are prioritized
- Travel is planned during less busy periods

BUDGET RECOMMENDATION

For student travellers, budget-friendly and balanced travel styles can help control expenses.

For families, the budget should consider accommodation type, number of travellers, transportation and child-friendly activities.

For groups, shared transportation and group accommodation can reduce the per-person cost.

The final budget should always be treated as an estimate because actual prices can change based on season, availability and travel choices.
